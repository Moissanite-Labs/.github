Lab / research

Small harnesses,
under selection.

Razi cells is a research program at Moissanite Labs. A cell is a very small agent harness that starts from a need and an empty folder, writes its own tools, and is kept or dropped by a measured score. The program asks which laws, felt from inside by each cell, make a colony improve on its own. Everything here is exploratory. Guesses are written down before the data, and misses are reported.
