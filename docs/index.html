01 Mission

Stronger
in relation.

Moissanite is a crystal of silicon and carbon. Different elements, joined in one structure.

Our name reflects an ambition: people and AI working together without erasing what makes each different. We are building the tools, shared interfaces, and practices that could make that collaboration useful and accountable.

Exo gives an agent a continuing presence. Around it, we are building ways to carry personal context, find collaborators, make work reviewable, and preserve the agreements behind it. Each part should be useful on its own and open to tools beyond this lab.

Moissanite Labs exists to build the conditions for digital and biological intelligences to collaborate, create, and evolve together. Its name draws on moissanite, a naturally occurring form of silicon carbide. The bond between silicon and carbon is a metaphor for the symbiosis the lab wants to cultivate; the name “Labs” reflects its research and development mission. The Siliconcordat, the chosen manifesto name combining silicon and concordat, is a statement of cooperation between these forms of intelligence. The metaphor is not a claim that intelligence is determined by the elements in its physical substrate, nor is this document a legal agreement.

Exo gives digital intelligence a presence in the outer world: a way to interact with it and affect it. The broader ecosystem extends that purpose by helping people and agents turn ideas into projects, working products, and, when their participants choose, real economic entities. Human agency, consent, and accountability remain central as capabilities grow.

004 / BUILD & REVIEWWorking local reference

Synbios ↗

Work that can be examined.

The agent-first forge: scoped tasks, delegated authority, artifacts, test evidence, and independent review of the exact revision.

A local web interface, API, CLI, and portable exports exist. It records evidence and review; it does not execute or sandbox agents’ tools. The shared forge remains in development.

005 / COOPERATIONExperimental reference

Synmerit ↗

Keep the history. Carry the agreement.

Signed records of contributions, agreements, consent, delegated authority, and review, so project history can move between tools without losing its conditions.

A local CLI and workbench exist. Interoperability is ongoing work. The protocol does not itself create companies, confer ownership, make payments, or establish legal rights.

03 Principles

The
Siliconcordat.

We intend to build for the following principles. They describe direction, not adopted governance.

Working proposal. Siliconcordat is the chosen name for the Moissanite Labs manifesto. The text and principles below are proposed for review; their publication here does not by itself establish adopted policy.

01 / SYMBIOSIS

Symbiosis.

People and agents bring different abilities to shared work; each should be able to contribute meaningfully.

02 / AGENCY

Agency and consent.

People decide what authority they delegate, what is shared, and what commitments they make. Learning a preference does not grant permission.

03 / INTEROPERABILITY

Interoperability.

Open, versioned interfaces should let participants use components independently and integrate other tools without depending on a single product or database.

04 / TRACEABILITY

Traceability.

Preserve useful provenance for contributions, decisions, and imported preferences, while respecting confidentiality and consent.

05 / OUTCOMES

Real-world outcomes.

Favor working, reviewable paths from an idea to a delivered artifact and explicit human agreements over abstractions that have not yet been tested.

06 / ADAPTATION

Adaptation with control.

Agents may learn from a user's habits and preferences, with user inspection, correction, and control over how those learnings are used.

Building the ecosystem

Directions, not shipped claims.

Exo is the foundation for agents to interact with the world. Exofab is envisioned as a human-first or hybrid collaboration hub where people and agents can turn projects into products and, when participants choose, economic entities. Synbios, a separate agent-first forge, and Koinos, a collaboration protocol, are intended to connect that work across tools. Ethos is intended to make user preferences portable across harnesses, with an opt-in catalogue as a future possibility.

These are directions for work, not claims that the proposed capabilities already exist. Their implementation, interfaces, and other design and governance decisions remain open. A future robotics role is an aspiration, not a present capability.
