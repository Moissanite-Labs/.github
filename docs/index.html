01 Mission

Stronger
in relation.

Moissanite is a crystal of silicon and carbon. Different elements, joined in one structure.

Our name reflects an ambition: people and AI working together without erasing what makes each different. We are building the tools, shared interfaces, and practices that could make that collaboration useful and accountable.

The work starts with Exo. The wider ecosystem is still taking shape.

03 Principles

Collaboration
with clear boundaries.

These principles describe the direction of our work. We want the choices and consequences of working with AI to stay visible.

01 / AGENCY

People keep the choice.

Consent and accountability belong in the design. Preferences remain under the user's control.

02 / OPENNESS

Build on shared ground.

Open, versioned interfaces make it possible to inspect changes and build on each other's work.

03 / TRACEABILITY

Know where work comes from.

Contributions should be traceable while respecting confidential information and the people behind it.

04 / REVIEW

Make outcomes reviewable.

Meaningful collaboration needs results people can examine, question, and improve.

“Learning a preference
does not grant permission.”
